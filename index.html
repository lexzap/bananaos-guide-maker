BANANAOS Guide Maker

Walkthroughs for the Guide on BananaOS's second screen, made in this browser from StrategyWiki and free game wikis. Name your games, get one zip, copy it onto the card. While a game plays, its guide opens at the level you're on.

1Name your games

One per line. The game's file name is best (Super Mario Bros. 2 (USA).nes): the Guide finds a guide by it. Pick the system the games are in.

About 15 seconds a game: the wikis are asked one page at a time.

2What was found

StrategyWiki first (walkthroughs), then the game's franchise wiki (the Super Mario Wiki, Zelda Wiki and others: encyclopedia pages per level), only where the wiki's licence is free (CC BY-SA). Text only.

3Put them on the card

The zip holds banana/guides/<system>/<game>/. Copy its banana folder onto the card's big partition, merging folders (File sharing, or a card reader). Then in a game: Guide, beside All achievements.
